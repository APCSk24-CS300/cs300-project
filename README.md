# myHCMUS

**A Web-Based Platform for Extracurricular Activity Management and Student Training Scores**

## Introduction

**myHCMUS** is a web-based platform designed to simplify the management of extracurricular activities and student training scores at HCMUS.

The platform allows students to discover and register for extracurricular activities, track their participation, monitor their training scores, and access university services through a centralized dashboard. It also provides organizers and administrators with tools to manage activities, registrations, attendance, participation records, and system-wide information efficiently.

By bringing these services together in one platform, myHCMUS aims to improve the student experience, reduce administrative workload, and make extracurricular activity management more transparent and accessible.

## Target Users

myHCMUS serves the following user groups:

- **Students:** Discover activities, register for events, track participation, monitor training scores, and access university services.
- **Organizers:** Create and manage activities, review registrations, record attendance, verify participation, and handle student appeals.
- **Administrators:** Manage activity categories, configure system settings, and monitor platform-wide statistics and reports.

## Key Features

### 1. Authentication and Authorization
- Log in and log out securely.
- Identify user roles and provide role-based access to system features.
- Support appropriate access for students, organizers, and administrators.

### 2. Profile Management
- Update personal information and avatars.
- Change account passwords.
- Manage basic academic information.

### 3. Activity Discovery
- Browse available extracurricular activities.
- Search and filter activities by category, faculty, date, location, training-score criteria, and registration status.
- View activity descriptions, schedules, capacity, eligibility requirements, and registration deadlines.

### 4. Activity Registration Management
- Register for activities and cancel registrations within the permitted period.
- Track registration status.
- Allow organizers to review participant lists and approve or reject applications when necessary.
- Manage waiting lists for activities with limited capacity.

### 5. Activity Management
- Create, edit, and manage extracurricular activities.
- Configure schedules, locations, participation requirements, capacity, and training-score criteria.
- Publish announcements and activity updates.
- Track activity statistics and export reports.

### 6. Training Score Management
- View accumulated training scores by semester and evaluation criteria.
- Review participation history and related score records.
- Allow organizers to submit verified participation results and make appropriate score adjustments.

### 7. Calendar Management
- Display registered activities in a personal calendar.
- Identify scheduling conflicts between activities.
- Help students plan their participation more effectively.

### 8. Notification Management
- Receive reminders about upcoming activities and registration deadlines.
- Get updates on registration status and schedule changes.
- Stay informed about important activity announcements.

### 9. Appeal Management
- Submit appeals regarding missing or incorrect participation records and training scores.
- Allow organizers to review appeals and provide responses.
- Track appeal status and update cases when resolved.

### 10. System Administration
- Manage activity categories and system-wide settings.
- Access reports on activity participation and registration trends.
- Monitor training-score distribution and other platform statistics.

### 11. University Services Hub
- Access commonly used university platforms from a centralized dashboard.
- Provide quick links to services such as Moodle, the Student Portal, and tuition payment systems.

## AI Feature

### AI Student Assistant

The AI Student Assistant helps students discover extracurricular activities that match their interests and needs.

It provides personalized activity recommendations based on factors such as:

- Student interests and preferences.
- Personal schedules and activity availability.
- Previous participation history.
- Training-score requirements and goals.

The assistant aims to reduce the time students spend searching for activities and help them make informed decisions about extracurricular participation.

## Supported Environments

myHCMUS is designed to be accessible through modern web browsers on:

- Desktop computers.
- Laptops.
- Mobile devices.

The interface aims to provide a convenient experience across different screen sizes.

## Project Goals

The main goals of myHCMUS are to:

- Centralize extracurricular activity information and university service links.
- Simplify activity discovery, registration, and participation tracking.
- Improve the transparency and accessibility of student training scores.
- Support organizers in managing activities and student records efficiently.
- Provide administrators with useful reports and system management tools.
- Enhance student decision-making through personalized AI recommendations.
